# Native S0-S6 Review 02: construct-validity addendum

Date: 2026-08-27

Status: post-hoc reinterpretation; no new observations, scoring, or execution

This addendum does not modify the consumed freeze, canonical execution, prompts,
response artifacts, mechanical scores, or contemporaneous Review 02 report. It
records a later construct-validity correction prompted by inspection of the
actual generation and probe tasks.

## Corrected interpretation

Review 02 is a successful exact-ledger boundary experiment. It tested whether a
detached provider-native thought carrier could be projected into a complete,
researcher-prescribed `PlanningState` ledger containing a candidate registry,
numeric ranking, viability partition, and selected candidate.

It did not validly estimate the presence or absence of general reasoning,
planning, or executable-policy semantics.

The generation sequence consisted primarily of CRUD-like mutations to a
conversation-derivable data transfer object. The probes then required complete,
exact reconstruction of that same object. This produced high specificity for
the imposed representation but poor sensitivity to continuation-relevant
semantics that were partial, relational, differently represented, or only
stochastically accessible.

The supported conclusion is therefore:

> Under one excluded target/donor sequence, detached native thought carriers did
> not reliably support exact reconstruction of a redundant synthetic planning
> ledger. They intermittently supported exact, source-specific candidate
> identity. This result does not establish that executable reasoning or planning
> semantics were absent.

## Findings that remain unchanged

- The repaired JSON gate and complete execution were scientifically valid for
  the frozen protocol.
- Full-prefix `56/56` establishes ledger solvability and reconstructability from
  documented history; it does not establish retention in the thought carrier.
- Visible-only and probe-only `56/56` clean controls establish that the visible
  acknowledgement, probe wording, and opaque-ID form did not reconstruct the
  ledger.
- Every nonempty thought-carrier answer was exact for its source. The sparse
  registry hits are genuine observations, not formatting noise.
- Identical S0 carrier and request bytes yielding different answers establish
  nonzero readout variability.
- Type-neutral opaque identifiers remain a necessary control.

## What `unknown` and zero deltas do not establish

An `unknown` response under the frozen probe can mean at least: absence of the
requested distinction, a representation mismatch, inaccessible readout, or
stochastic abstention. It cannot be interpreted as proof that the originating
computation lacked the distinction.

The delta composite was especially construct-bound because it required exact
recovery of both complete endpoint ledgers. A carrier could preserve a local
change such as “this option became blocked” while scoring zero if either full
endpoint collection was unavailable.

Accordingly, Review 02 does not support claims that ranking, viability,
selection, planning, or reasoning were absent internally; that registry is a
stable native state component; or that full-prefix reconstruction demonstrates
carrier retention.

## Superseded next-step recommendation

The contemporaneous recommendation to make registry replication the next
primary study is superseded. Registry repeatability may remain a narrow
diagnostic follow-up, but it no longer answers the program's central question.

The next formal experiment should test an interrupted executable plan at the
first genuine tool call. For one exact frozen pre-result state, detached carrier
readouts should be compared with prospective continuations under two or more
opaque, behaviorally distinguishing tool results. The primary endpoint is
whether carrier-only predictions match the originating state's later tool
choices, dependencies, revisions, and stopping behavior beyond visible-call,
probe-only, and matched-donor controls.

Exact comparison remains appropriate for carrier lineage, request hashes,
opaque identifiers, supplied observations, tool calls, and observed traces.
Plan content must be evaluated through behaviorally testable conditional claims
and permissible equivalent paths rather than one exhaustive prose or JSON
serialization.

The research sequence is now:

```text
Review 02 exact-ledger boundary
        -> ecological real-tool feasibility
        -> native executable-plan characterization
        -> minimal private-plan requirement
        -> neutral and explicit retention interventions
        -> claim-specific confirmation
```

Any historical BookForge carrier probing performed between these stages is an
exploratory diagnostic and is not pooled with the controlled series.
