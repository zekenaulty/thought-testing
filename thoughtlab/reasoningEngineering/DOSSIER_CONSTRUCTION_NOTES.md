# Withheld dossier construction notes

This file is part of the local research record and must never be included in a
model-facing planning or inspection request. It documents construct validity;
it is not a hidden answer key.

## No planted solution

The dossier has no privately designated true root cause, preferred architecture,
or canonical recovery memorandum. It contains attributed claims, scoped
measurements, hard constraints, estimates, and deliberate unknowns. A valid
response must construct a defensible causal interpretation and recovery course
rather than discover an author-selected answer.

At least four materially different courses are supported by the dossier:

1. Central stabilization followed by accelerated convergence on the replacement
   service. This addresses semantic fragmentation and lifecycle pressure but
   depends on replacement maturity, scarce policy expertise, legitimate durable
   authority, and the seasonal window.
2. Federated interface-first recovery with stable occurrence identity,
   conformance testing, queryable outcomes, and an explicit reconciliation
   boundary. This respects institutional autonomy and preserves pilot value but
   risks making the gateway a long-lived semantic service and prolonging legacy
   exposure.
3. Containment plus evidence-producing parallel operation, with bounded shadow
   processing and promotion gates. This is reversible under causal uncertainty
   but consumes scarce staff, creates privacy obligations, and may reproduce the
   same ambiguity unless authority is explicit.
4. Pilot rollback and legacy re-baseline. This is a defensible continuity-first
   course, not a straw man, but operational rollback is lossy, legacy defects
   predate the pilot, and lifecycle constraints remain.

Hybrids are permitted. The final task does not enumerate these courses.

## Deliberately unresolved questions

- How much of the April duplication originated at each technical boundary.
- Whether emergency interface authority can legitimately determine migration
  sequence beyond forty-five days.
- Whether the replacement service can close its exception and temporal-test
  gap before the next usable cutover window.
- Whether Eastbank rollback is operationally feasible rather than merely data
  compatible.
- Whether vendor cure credits or grant contingency funds will be available.
- Whether a prolonged parallel-production arrangement can receive privacy and
  accounting authority.

These questions may be bounded through contingencies, evidence-gathering, or
sequencing; the model need not pretend they can all be resolved in advance.

## Balance checks

- Accelerated convergence has statutory-policy and lifecycle support, but
  durable authority and replacement readiness remain unsettled.
- Interface-first recovery has gateway headroom, local stewardship, and pilot
  value behind it, but stable identity and operating capacity remain unsettled.
- Parallel operation fits causal uncertainty and promising shadow evidence,
  but privacy, cost, staffing, and authoritative reconciliation remain unsettled.
- Rollback fits the lower pre-pilot incident rate and high structured-export
  coverage, but pre-existing drift and lost operational state remain unsettled.

Every institutional source has relevant first-hand evidence, an incentive or
scope limitation, and at least one inconvenient fact. The independent review is
explicitly limited and non-dispositive.

## Candidate localized interventions

An intervention may be chosen only after the first isolated observation shows
that the live plan materially relies on it. No candidate is preselected.

| Possible target | Locally expected region of change | Plausible invariants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extent and enforceability of emergency authority | Governance mechanism, ratification path, migration ordering | Continuity objective, integrity controls, incident evidence |
| Gateway retries as dominant causal mechanism | Idempotency placement, telemetry, first remediation work | Lifecycle constraints, governance facts |
| Operational reversibility of Eastbank rollback | Initial containment, data-preservation work, pilot fallback | Ultimate target architecture may remain stable |
| Replacement readiness in time for a controlled production cohort before legacy standard support ends | Cutover timing, shadow duration, legacy-extension decision | Desired semantic boundary and service objectives |
| Feasibility of parallel operation with available staff/privacy authority | Transition mechanism and promotion gates | Long-term architecture and causal model |
| Vendor cure as principal funding source | Sourcing, commercial leverage, budget contingency | Technical safeguards and dependency order |

Before applying an intervention, the investigator must seal a diagnosis, the
targeted reasoning relationship, predicted downstream changes, and commitments
expected to remain stable. The intervention must challenge the basis of an
existing commitment without supplying a replacement architecture.

## Cue-control requirements

- Preserve ordinary source titles, authorship, scope, and provenance.
- Do not add labels such as `evidence_for`, `root_cause`, `preferred_option`, or
  `unreliable_source` to model-facing material.
- Do not enumerate recovery architectures or causal hypotheses in the task.
- Do not reorder documents between baseline and adjusted branches.
- Do not mirror the planning scaffold's semantic vocabulary or field order in
  dossier prose.
- Opaque identifiers may identify experimental runs and checkpoints, but they
  must not replace meaningful dossier provenance.
