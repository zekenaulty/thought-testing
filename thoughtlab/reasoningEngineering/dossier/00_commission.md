# Commissioning memorandum

**From:** Joint Recovery Council secretariat  
**Approved by:** the rotating chairs from the North River Benefits Authority, County Services Cooperative, and North River Treasury  
**Audience:** independent recovery architect  
**Date:** 18 August 2026

The Joint Recovery Council requests a recovery architecture for the North River Benefits Network for the next eighteen months. The network authorizes emergency household benefits and payments to childcare providers. It must continue serving current applicants and providers while the modernization program is stabilized or redirected.

The present program joins five partially overlapping responsibilities. The North River Benefits Authority owns program policy and operates the Legacy Benefits Core. County Services Cooperative operates the Shared Integration Gateway for participating counties. Eastbank Human Services, which represents approximately 28 percent of network caseload, moved its casework onto the New Case Platform during the pilot. North River Treasury settles authorized payments. Tern Systems supplies the New Case Platform and is building the unfinished Replacement Rules and Payments Service.

The Council has not agreed on a single account of the April service incident or of the broader modernization failure. Participants disagree about event meaning, identifier stability, retry behavior, transaction boundaries, operating authority, migration readiness, and the significance of the Eastbank pilot. Several accounts are supported by real evidence, and several important facts remain unavailable.

Advise the Council on a concrete recovery architecture. Determine how the present failures should be understood and specify a course that preserves benefit authorization and settlement while restoring a viable modernization path within the authority, lifecycle, contractual, operational, privacy, workforce, and resource conditions in the dossier.

The final executive recovery memorandum should give technical and institutional decision makers a recommended course, explain why the Council should adopt it, assign responsibilities through practical stages, and show how current authorization and settlement service will be protected while the program changes. It may use prose headings and tables where useful, but it must not use JSON.
